1. What Odometer Does

Odometer is a GPS-powered speedometer and trip-tracking app for Android and iOS. When you start a drive, it uses your device's GPS to measure your speed in real time and display it on an analog gauge, a digital readout, or a live map. At the end of each trip, it saves statistics — distance traveled, maximum speed, average speed, and duration — locally on your device so you can review your journey history.

The App is not a navigation app, a route planner, or a law-enforcement tool. It is a speed-monitoring and trip-logging tool only.

2. Information We Collect

Odometer does not transmit location data or trip history to our servers. All data the App creates stays on your device, except for interactions with Google AdMob as described in Section 8.

2.1 Location & GPS Data

When the App is in use (and when you grant background location permission on Android), Odometer reads your device's GPS to determine:

  • Current speed — used to display the real-time speedometer reading.
  • Coordinates — used to display your position on the in-app map view. Coordinates are never stored in trip logs or transmitted.
  • GPS accuracy — used to filter out unreliable readings (speeds reported with accuracy worse than 50 m are discarded).

GPS data is processed in memory only while the App is running. We do not log, upload, or share your GPS coordinates.

2.2 Trip Statistics

When you end a drive session, the App saves the following trip summary locally on your device:

  • Trip start and end time
  • Total distance traveled
  • Maximum speed reached
  • Average speed
  • Trip duration

This data is stored in your device's local storage and is accessible only within the App. It is never uploaded to any server.

2.3 App Settings

Your chosen preferences — speed unit (km/h, mph, knots), display theme, accent color, language, analog gauge range, vehicle mode, and other settings — are stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere.

2.4 Billing Information

If you purchase a premium subscription or in-app purchase, payment is processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. We may receive subscription status and anonymous purchase identifiers to unlock premium features.

3. How We Use Your Information

5. Permissions

Odometer requests the following permissions:

  • Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Required to receive accurate GPS speed and position readings from your device. Without this, the speedometer cannot function.
  • Background Location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION — Android): Requested so the App can continue tracking your speed and distance when your screen is off or when you switch to another app. You may deny this; speed tracking will only work while the App is in the foreground.
  • Location Services (iOS): "Allow While Using App" is required for foreground tracking. "Always Allow" enables background speed tracking.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Keeps the GPS speed service running reliably on Android while you drive. Android requires a visible notification when a foreground service is active.
  • Foreground Service Type — Location (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): Required by Android 14+ to declare that our foreground service uses location data.
  • Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — Android): Required for the optional Floating Window mode, which displays a small speedometer overlay on top of other apps while you drive. This permission is only requested if you enable Window Mode in Settings.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): Used for the optional speed alarm, which vibrates your device when your speed exceeds your configured maximum.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — Android): Requests that the device not stop the GPS service in the background. You can deny this; the App will still work but background tracking may be interrupted on battery-aggressive devices.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Android 13+): Required to show the foreground service notification while background tracking is active.

6. Foreground Service Notification

When background speed tracking is active on Android, a persistent notification is shown in your notification drawer. This is a mandatory Android requirement for foreground services. The notification indicates that GPS tracking is running and provides a way to stop it. It contains no personal data.
