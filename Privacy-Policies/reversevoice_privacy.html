About the App

Reverse Singing & Voice Studio lets you record your voice or import a sound, play it backwards, and take on fun reverse challenges alone or with friends on one phone.

Key Features

  • Record & reverse: Record your voice with the microphone (30 seconds free, 60 seconds with Premium) or import an audio file, and hear it played backwards.
  • Playback controls: Change the speed and pitch, loop the sound and compare the original with the reversed version.
  • Voice filters: Apply simple sound effects to your recording.
  • Reverse challenges: Listen to a reversed word, then say it backwards and try to guess the answer. Play solo through levels and packs, or in Party mode with friends on the same device.
  • History: Save your recordings on your device, then play, rename, share or delete them.
  • Save & share: Export a recording and share it with the apps you choose.
  • Languages: The app is available in English (US and UK), German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Hindi, Urdu and Arabic. Challenge content and voices are in English.

How It Works

Reverse Singing & Voice Studio works on your device. Recording, reversing, filters and challenges are processed locally, there is no account and there is no server that stores your recordings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Challenge words are spoken with your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. The App is independent and is not affiliated with any music, social media or voice service.
