Privacy Policy

TV Remote ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use the App to control your Android TV or Google TV over your Wi-Fi network.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require account creation, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

If you contact us:

  • Email address
  • Any information you provide

b. TV & Local Network Data

To find and control your TV, the App uses:

  • The names, brands, models, and local IP addresses of Android TV / Google TV devices found on your Wi-Fi network
  • A pairing certificate created on your phone the first time you pair with a TV, so the TV recognizes your phone on later connections
  • Your saved TVs, favorites, and the time a TV was last connected

Note:

  • This data is stored only on your device
  • It is never sent to us or to any server we operate
  • It stays on your device until you forget a TV, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App

c. Remote Commands & Text Input

When you use the App:

  • Button presses (power, D-pad, volume, channels, number pad, etc.) are sent directly to your TV
  • Text you type with the keyboard feature is sent directly to your TV
  • App launch requests are sent directly to your TV

Note:

  • Commands and text travel over your local network over an encrypted connection to the TV
  • We do not collect, store, or read your commands or typed text

d. App Preferences

The App stores on your device:

  • Haptic and sound feedback settings
  • Auto-reconnect setting
  • Appearance (light / dark / system)
  • Whether you have completed onboarding

e. Usage Data

The App does not include analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools, and does not collect usage data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Discover compatible TVs on your Wi-Fi network
  • Pair with and reconnect to your TV
  • Send remote-control commands, text, and app launch requests to your TV
  • Remember your saved TVs and preferences
  • Respond to support queries

3. Permissions

  • Wi-Fi & Network State: To check that your phone is connected to Wi-Fi and to find TVs on the same network.
  • Wi-Fi Multicast: To discover TVs on your local network (mDNS / DIAL).
  • Internet / Network Access: To connect to your TV over your local network.

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location.

4. Advertising

The App does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers.

If advertising is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released, describing the advertising partners used and how you can opt out of personalized ads.

5. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with:

  • Your Android TV / Google TV, using the Android TV Remote protocol on your local network
  • Streaming apps installed on your TV (such as Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+), which the App opens through their links
  • Android's built-in Cast / screen-mirroring feature, which the App opens but does not control

Once an app is opened on your TV, or while you use Android's screen mirroring, that app or feature operates under its own privacy policy.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers:

  • Google (Android TV, Google TV & Google Play): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Your TV manufacturer's privacy policy, for data your TV itself collects

6. Data Security

We implement:

  • Encrypted (TLS) connections between your phone and your TV
  • PIN-based pairing, so only phones you approve on the TV can control it
  • A pairing key protected with the Android Keystore on your device
  • Minimal data collection and no server-side storage

To show the names of discovered TVs, the App may read device descriptions that TVs publish over plain HTTP on your local network. This information never leaves your local network.

7. Data Retention

  • Saved TVs, pairing data, and preferences are kept on your device until you forget a TV, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App
  • Remote commands and typed text are not stored
  • Emails may be retained for support

8. Compatibility Disclaimer

The App works only with Android TV and Google TV devices that support the Android TV Remote service. Some features (such as app launching, number keys, or text input) depend on your TV model and the apps installed on it, and may not work on every TV. Protected streaming apps may appear black when screen mirroring; this is a limitation of those apps and of Android, not of the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age unless permitted by applicable law and under parental supervision.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Because the App stores its data only on your device, you are in control of it. You may:

  • Forget a saved TV from the App's Home screen
  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App
  • Request access to or deletion of any support emails you have sent us

Contact: support@appaxislab.com

11. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected with a new effective date.

support@appaxislab.com
