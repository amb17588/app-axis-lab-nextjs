About the App

QuickLens: QR & Barcode Reader is a fast, on-device tool for scanning and creating QR codes. Point your camera at a code to scan it instantly, or generate your own QR codes to share.

Key Features

  • QR & barcode scanning: Scan QR codes and common barcodes instantly using your camera.
  • Scan from photo: Pick an existing image from your photo library to decode a QR code in it.
  • QR code generator: Create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts and more, and save them to your photo library.
  • Flashlight toggle: Turn on the camera flash to scan in low light.
  • Scan & generate history: Keeps a local log of your past scans and created codes, stored only on your device.
  • Premium features: Remove ads, unlock unlimited history, remove the watermark on generated codes, and access advanced QR customization (colors, logos and export options).

How It Works

QuickLens: QR & Barcode Reader uses your device's camera to scan codes and, where you choose, your photo library to scan an existing image or save a generated code. Everything is processed on your device. There is no account, and the content of your scans, generated codes and history is never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Scanning accuracy depends on camera quality, lighting and the condition of the code being scanned. The App is independent and is not affiliated with any third-party service whose link or content you may scan.
