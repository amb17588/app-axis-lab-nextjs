Privacy Policy

LingoLab: AI Language Tutor ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use the App to learn and practice languages, including speaking practice, lessons, quizzes, translation, and chat with an AI tutor.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require account creation, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

If you contact us:

  • Email address
  • Any information you provide

b. Learning Progress & Preferences

To personalize your experience, the App stores on your device:

  • Your selected app language and learning (target) language
  • Your Translate screen language pair
  • Whether you have completed onboarding
  • Your Learning Path progress, completed steps, gems, and daily missions and streaks
  • Your premium (subscription) status
  • The number of free AI chat messages you have used today

Note:

  • This data is stored only on your device
  • It stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App

c. Voice & Speaking Practice

When you use speaking practice, the Speaking Quiz, Job Interview practice, or the microphone button in Chat:

  • The App uses your device's speech recognition to convert what you say into text, so it can compare it with the expected phrase
  • The App does not record, store, or upload your audio
  • Speech recognition is provided by your device's operating system (for example, Apple's or Google's speech services), and your device may process it on-device or through that provider under its own privacy policy

d. AI Features (Quiz Feedback, Chat with AI & Translate)

When you use AI features, the App sends text only to our AI provider (OpenAI) to produce a response:

  • Quiz feedback: the expected phrases and the text that was recognized from your speech
  • Chat with AI: the messages in your current chat session (up to the last 24 turns)
  • Translate: the text you enter and the selected languages

Note:

  • Audio is never sent
  • Chat conversations live only in the App's memory while the screen is open and are not saved by the App; leaving the screen starts a fresh session next time
  • We do not ask for personal information in these features; please avoid entering sensitive personal information into chat or translation

e. Learning Content

Lessons, quizzes, passages, and vocabulary are downloaded from our content service (Supabase). These requests fetch learning content only; they use no account and do not send your personal information. Your IP address may be visible to the service as part of any normal internet request.

f. Usage, Diagnostics & Device Data

The App uses Google Firebase services to understand how the App is used and to keep it stable:

  • Firebase Analytics: screen views and actions such as lessons completed or language changed
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and error details, including device model, operating system version, and App version
  • Firebase Remote Config: settings that control App behavior, such as which screens or ads are shown

These services may use an app-instance identifier and may collect general device and approximate-location (derived from IP address) information. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

g. Purchases

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status and an anonymous purchase identifier. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide lessons, quizzes, speaking practice, translation, and AI chat
  • Check your pronunciation and give feedback on your practice
  • Remember your progress, language choices, and preferences
  • Manage subscriptions and unlock premium features
  • Show advertisements
  • Analyze usage and fix crashes to improve the App
  • Respond to support queries

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: To hear your speech during speaking practice and dictation.
  • Speech Recognition (iOS): To convert your speech into text for pronunciation checking.
  • Internet / Network Access: To download learning content, use AI features, show ads, and process purchases.
  • Billing (Android): To process subscriptions through Google Play.

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, photos, or precise location. You can use the App without granting the microphone permission, but speaking features will not work.

4. Advertising

The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob to users without an active premium subscription. Ads include banner ads, native ads, and full-screen (interstitial) ads shown at app start and after the subscription screen closes.

AdMob may use advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or the iOS IDFA) and device information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and may show personalized ads where permitted. You can limit personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings

Subscribing to premium removes ads.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each operating under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • OpenAI (AI feedback, chat, and translation, text only): https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Supabase (learning content): https://supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases): Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy
  • Your device's speech recognition and text-to-speech services (Apple / Google)

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Security

We implement:

  • Encrypted (HTTPS) connections for content, AI requests, and purchases
  • Text-only AI requests; audio is never uploaded
  • No chat history saved by the App
  • Minimal data collection, no accounts, and learning progress kept on your device

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

  • Progress, gems, preferences, and settings are kept on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App
  • Chat messages are not stored by the App; text sent to OpenAI is handled under OpenAI's retention policy
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to its retention settings
  • Emails may be retained for support

8. AI & Learning Disclaimer

AI-generated feedback, chat replies, and translations may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for professional, legal, medical, or official use. Pronunciation scoring depends on your device's speech recognition and may vary with accent, noise, and device. LingoLab is an educational aid and does not guarantee any exam result, including IELTS or job interviews.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age unless permitted by applicable law and under parental supervision.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Because the App stores your progress only on your device, you are in control of it. You may:

  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App
  • Limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Deny or revoke the microphone and speech recognition permissions in your device settings
  • Cancel your subscription in the Apple App Store or Google Play settings
  • Request access to or deletion of any support emails you have sent us
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, or California), exercise additional rights under local privacy laws by contacting us

Contact: support@appaxislab.com

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected with a new effective date.
