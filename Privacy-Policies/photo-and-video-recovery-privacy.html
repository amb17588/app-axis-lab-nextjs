Privacy Policy

Photos & Videos Recovery ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use the App to recover deleted photos, videos, and audio files from your device.

1 Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require account creation, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

If you contact us for support, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • Any information you choose to provide

b. Media & Storage Data

To scan and recover deleted files, the App accesses:

  • Your device's media library — photos, videos, and audio files you grant access to
  • File metadata such as file name, size, type, and modification date
  • Thumbnails generated locally for recovered file previews
Note:
  • All media scanning and recovery happens entirely on your device
  • Your photos, videos, and audio files are never uploaded to us or any server we operate
  • Recovery results are stored only on your device and cleared when you uninstall the App

c. App Preferences

The App stores on your device:

  • Scan history and recovered file records (stored in a local database)
  • Appearance settings (light / dark / system)
  • Whether you have completed onboarding
  • Language and localization preferences

d. Usage & Crash Data

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. These services may collect:

  • Anonymous usage events (screens visited, features used)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Device type, OS version, and App version
Note: This data is anonymized and aggregated. Your actual media files, photos, or videos are never included in these reports.

2 How We Use Information

  • Scan your device's media library to find and recover deleted photos, videos, and audio
  • Display previews and thumbnails of recoverable files
  • Save recovered files to your chosen location on your device
  • Remember your scan history and preferences
  • Improve App performance and fix bugs using crash and usage data
  • Respond to support queries

3 Permissions

  • Read Media (Photos / Videos / Audio): Required to scan your device's media library and display recoverable files. Requested via Android's granular media permissions.
  • Read External Storage (Android 9 and below): Required to access media files on older Android versions.
  • Internet / Network Access: Used for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and ads (if applicable).

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

4 Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising identifier (GAID) and other non-personally-identifiable signals to show relevant ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5 Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Google): Usage analytics and crash reporting
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): Remote feature configuration
  • Google AdMob: Advertising
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase and subscription management

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers:

  • Google / Firebase: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

6 Data Security

  • All media scanning and recovery is processed locally on your device
  • No media files, photos, or videos are ever transmitted to our servers
  • Local database (SQLite) stores only file metadata and scan records — not your actual media content
  • Communication with Firebase and AdMob services uses encrypted (TLS) connections
  • Minimal data collection — we only collect what is necessary for the App to function

7 Data Retention

  • Scan history and app preferences are stored on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App
  • Your media files are never stored or retained by us
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention policies
  • Support emails may be retained for a reasonable period to resolve your query

8 Recovery Disclaimer

The App can only recover files that have not yet been permanently overwritten by your device's storage system. Recovery success depends on your device model, Android version, storage type, and how much time has passed since deletion. We cannot guarantee that all deleted files will be recoverable.

9 Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age unless permitted by applicable law and under parental supervision.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

10 Your Privacy Rights

Because the App stores its data only on your device, you are in full control of it. You may:

  • Clear scan history from within the App
  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App
  • Revoke media permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Photos & Videos Recovery → Permissions
  • Request access to or deletion of any support emails you have sent us
