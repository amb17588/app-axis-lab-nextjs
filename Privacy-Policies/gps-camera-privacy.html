Privacy Policy

GPS Camera ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use GPS Camera to take geotagged photos, explore maps, measure areas, check air quality, and use the other features of the App.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require account creation, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

If you contact us for support, we may receive:

  • Your email address
  • Any information you choose to include in your message

b. Location Data

GPS Camera uses your device's GPS and location services to power its core features, including:

  • Stamping photos and videos with GPS coordinates, address, altitude, speed, and compass bearing
  • Showing your current position on the map
  • Creating, saving, and navigating routes
  • Finding nearby places, parking, and points of interest
  • Measuring areas on the map
  • Displaying live traffic and Earth map layers
  • Air quality data for your current area
Note Location is accessed only while the App is in use (foreground). The App does not request background location access. GPS coordinates embedded in your photos are stored on your device as part of the photo file itself. We do not collect or transmit your location to our servers.

c. Camera & Microphone

The App uses your camera to capture photos and videos and your microphone to record audio with videos. Media you capture is saved to your device's photo library. We do not upload, access, or store your photos or videos on any server we operate.

d. Microphone — Voice Map

The Voice Map feature uses your microphone and on-device speech recognition to convert a spoken destination into a route. Speech recognition is handled by your device's operating system (Android or iOS). We do not record, transmit, or store your voice input.

e. Photo Library

The App reads your photo library to display images you have taken with GPS Camera in the Photo Grid. The App saves stamped photos and videos to your library. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

f. App Preferences & Settings

The App stores locally on your device:

  • Stamp layout preferences and customizations
  • Saved routes and waypoints
  • Map style preferences
  • Language selection
  • Whether you have completed onboarding
  • Subscription / purchase status

g. Advertising Identifier

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) to serve personalized or contextual ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

h. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. These services may collect:

  • Anonymous usage events (screens viewed, features used)
  • Device type, OS version, and App version
  • Crash reports and stack traces

This data is collected in aggregate and anonymized form. It is used solely to improve the App and is governed by Google's Firebase Privacy Policy .

2. How We Use Information

  • Embed GPS coordinates and metadata in your stamped photos and videos
  • Display your location on maps, routes, and navigation screens
  • Show nearby places, parking, and points of interest
  • Create and navigate routes, including voice-guided directions
  • Measure areas on the map
  • Provide traffic, live Earth, air quality, and compass features
  • Display personalized or contextual advertisements via AdMob
  • Diagnose crashes and improve App stability via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Analyze feature usage in aggregate to improve the App via Firebase Analytics
  • Respond to support queries

3. Permissions

  • Location (Foreground): To geotag photos, show your position on the map, create routes, and power all location-based features.
  • Camera: To capture geotagged photos and videos.
  • Microphone: To record audio with videos and to power Voice Map speech recognition.
  • Photo Library / Media: To display your captured photos in the Photo Grid and to save stamped photos and videos to your device.
  • Speech Recognition (iOS): To convert spoken destinations to routes in Voice Map.
  • Internet / Network Access: To load map tiles, fetch nearby places, air quality data, and serve advertisements.
  • Advertising ID (Android): Used by Google AdMob for ad serving.

You may deny permissions, but features that depend on them will not function.

4. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising identifier and general device information to show personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized ads:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"

Opting out does not remove ads; it limits their personalization.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps Platform — map tiles, geocoding, routes, and nearby places search
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting
  • Google Play In-App Purchases — subscription and premium feature billing (Android)
  • Apple App Store In-App Purchases — subscription and premium feature billing (iOS)

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Data Security

We implement the following safeguards:

  • All network requests use encrypted (HTTPS / TLS) connections
  • Location, photos, and preferences are stored only on your device
  • We do not operate servers that store your personal data, photos, or location history
  • Crash reports and analytics are anonymized before transmission to Firebase

7. Data Retention

  • Photos, videos, routes, and preferences are kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention schedule
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash logs are retained for 90 days per Google's policy
  • Support emails may be retained to resolve your query

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age unless permitted by applicable law and under parental supervision.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Because the App stores its data primarily on your device, you are in control of it. You may:

  • Delete photos, videos, and routes directly within the App
  • Reset or revoke app permissions at any time in your device settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (see Section 4)
  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in device settings or uninstalling it
  • Request access to or deletion of any support emails you have sent us

Contact: support@appaxislab.com
