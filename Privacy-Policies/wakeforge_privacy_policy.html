Privacy Policy

Scope

Welcome to WakeForge's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how WakeForge ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our mission-based alarm clock mobile application and related services (the "App" or "Services").

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Overview

WakeForge is the alarm that won't stop until you actually wake up — instead of a snooze button, you complete a short mini-game "mission" (math, memory, pattern, typing, tap, reps, or hold) to dismiss your alarm. It also offers optional social features so you can add friends, form groups, and set up shared alarms. WakeForge is built around an anonymous account — we don't ask for your name, email, or password to use the App.

  • No sign-up required — you get an anonymous account automatically; no email, phone number, or password is ever collected.
  • Your alarms stay on your device — personal alarms, mission history, streaks, and settings are stored locally and never leave your phone unless you use a shared alarm.
  • Social features are opt-in — if you choose to use friends, groups, or shared alarms, you pick a public display name, which is the only identifying information tied to your account.
  • We don't access your camera, contacts, or location — WakeForge does not request these device permissions, even though certain onboarding screens describe related concepts (see Section 5).
  • We show ads and use limited device data for them — via Google AdMob, never based on the content of your alarms, missions, or social connections.
  • We never sell your personal information.

1. Account & Setup

To use WakeForge, an anonymous account is created for you automatically the first time you open the App. Depending on how you use the App, this may also include:

  • A public display name you choose, only if you want to use friends, groups, or shared alarms
  • Preferences such as language, notification categories, and onboarding choices
  • Settings that control whether background activity, alarm-protection (Do Not Disturb bypass), and notification categories are enabled

These settings let us operate the App's core alarm features and, if you opt in, configure its social features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Anonymous Account Data

  • A randomly generated account identifier created via our backend provider, Supabase
  • No email address, phone number, or real name is collected to create this account

2.2 Profile & Social Data (only if you use Friends/Groups)

If you choose to set up a public identity to use friends, groups, or shared alarms, we store:

  • A unique display name you choose, and avatar initials derived from it
  • Friend requests, friendships, and blocks
  • Groups you create or join, and your membership/role within them
  • Shared alarms you create or are invited to — including time, mission, ringtone, and each participant's status
This data is visible only to people you're connected to — friends, group members, or people you invite. Access rules on our backend prevent other users from reading your data unless you're actually connected to them.

2.3 Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to our servers unless you explicitly share it via a shared alarm:

  • Your personal (non-shared) alarms — time, repeat days, chosen mission, ringtone, difficulty
  • Mission completion history, streaks, and win statistics
  • App preferences — language, notification categories, onboarding status, and the local preference toggles described in the onboarding flow (see Section 5)

2.4 Advertising & Device Information

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads. Google's SDK may collect and process data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • An advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and anonymous installation identifiers
  • Device type, model, operating system, and IP address
  • Ad interaction data

Our app requests non-personalized ads by default. This data is used to show ads and prevent fraud, and is never based on the content of your alarms, missions, or social connections.

2.5 What We Do Not Collect

  • No email address, phone number, or government ID
  • No precise or approximate location/GPS
  • No camera access or photos — the App's "camera"-tagged missions and onboarding screen are implemented without requesting real camera access (see Section 5)
  • No contacts list access
  • No microphone or audio recording
  • No payment card details — any purchases are billed entirely through the Google Play Store, which does not share your card details with us

2.6 Device Data

  • Device type, model, and operating system
  • App version and language/region settings
  • An advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and anonymous installation identifiers

2.7 Billing Information

If WakeForge offers a premium subscription or one-time purchase, payment is processed entirely by the Google Play Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. We may receive subscription status and anonymous purchase metadata from the Play Store to unlock premium features.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and operate your anonymous account
  • Provide core features — scheduling and ringing alarms, running missions, and tracking streaks and stats
  • Power the social features you opt into — friend requests, groups, and shared alarms
  • Sync shared-alarm schedules and participant status in real time across devices
  • Display and measure advertising
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App, including preventing abuse of the friend/group system
  • Process premium purchases through the Google Play Store, where offered
  • Provide customer support when you contact us
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use the content of your alarms, missions, or social connections for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

5. Permissions

WakeForge requests the following device capabilities:

  • Notifications: Optional, to send alarm reminders and the notification categories you enable. You can disable this in your device or app settings, though disabling it will prevent alarms from ringing.
  • Exact Alarms & Background Activity: Used to schedule alarms precisely, keep them running in the background, and let them ring even when your phone is idle, silent, or in Do Not Disturb mode. This also allows alarms to be rescheduled automatically after your device restarts.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to sync shared-alarm and social data, and to show ads.
WakeForge does not request Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or Location permissions. Certain onboarding screens describe camera- and activity-related concepts to explain planned or cosmetic app features (for example, a "camera" mission badge), but these screens only save a local on-device preference — no camera, contacts, or activity-recognition permission dialog is ever shown, and no such data is captured, transmitted, or stored by us.

6. Data Security

  • We minimize the data we collect and transmit to what is needed to provide the Services.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because your account is anonymous and cannot be recovered by email or password reset, you are responsible for keeping your device secure.
