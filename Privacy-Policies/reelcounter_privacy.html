About the App

ScrollPause: Reel Counter helps you take back your time from endless scrolling. It counts the reels and short videos you watch in the apps you choose, and gives you simple tools to cut back when you want to.

Key Features

  • Reel counter: Counts the Reels and Shorts you watch in YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Snapchat, per app and per day.
  • Floating counter bubble: A small bubble that shows your running count while you are inside the apps you chose.
  • Daily overview & reports: See today's total, a Healthy / Moderate / High status and a breakdown by app.
  • Reel Lock: Optionally lock Reels and Shorts so you cannot keep scrolling them.
  • App blocker: Block the apps you choose, with optional challenge screens (breathing, math or a written reason) before you can continue.
  • Website blocker: Block websites you add in supported browsers, plus an optional built-in 18+ block.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Urdu and Arabic.

How It Works

ScrollPause: Reel Counter uses Android's Accessibility Service and the display-over-other-apps permission, both of which you turn on yourself and can turn off at any time. Everything is processed on your device. There is no account, and your counts, block lists and browsing are never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Detection and blocking are best-effort and depend on how other apps are built. The App is independent and is not affiliated with YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat.

2. Accessibility Service: What It Reads and Why

The App uses Android's Accessibility Service API. You turn it on yourself in Android Settings, after an in-app disclosure that you must accept first. You can turn it off at any time.

While it is on, the App can see:

  • Which app is currently on screen
  • The on-screen layout of YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat, only to recognise when a Reels, Shorts or similar video screen is showing, so it can count the reels you watch or lock them if you turned Reel Lock on
  • The address-bar text of supported web browsers, only to compare it with the websites you chose to block and with the optional built-in 18+ list
  • When an app you chose to block is opened

This information is checked on your device at that moment. It is not recorded, not stored, not logged and not transmitted. The App does not read your messages, passwords, payment details or anything you type, and it does not keep a browsing history.

Usage Access (optional)

If you turn on the optional Usage access permission, the App also checks which app is in front, once a second, as a backup way to notice an app you chose to block. The app name is compared in memory only and is never stored or sent. This backup can block apps only, not websites or reels.

  • Notifications and foreground service: Keeps the floating counter running and shows its status notification.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs or files.
