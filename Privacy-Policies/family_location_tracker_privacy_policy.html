Privacy Policy

Scope

Welcome to Family Location Tracker's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Family Location Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our family safety and location-sharing mobile application and related services (the "App" or "Services").

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Overview

Family Location Tracker lets you create or join a private "circle" with family members or trusted contacts so you can see each other's real-time location, get alerted if someone is running low on battery or leaves/enters a chosen area, send an SOS to your circle in an emergency, and remotely ring a circle member's phone to help them find it. Because these features depend on knowing where your device is — including while the App is in the background — location is central to how Family Location Tracker works, and we've tried to be as specific as possible below about what is collected and why.

  • Location sharing is the core feature — we collect precise, continuous (including background) location for members of a circle so it can be shared with the other people in that circle.
  • Location is only visible to people in your circle — access rules on our backend prevent anyone else from seeing your location, and circle owners can hide their own location from a circle while still seeing others' locations.
  • You can sign in anonymously or with just an email — Family Location Tracker uses passwordless email sign-in (a one-time code) or an anonymous guest account. We never ask for or store a password.
  • Emergency (SOS) and "ring my device" features share your location and device state with your circle at the moment you trigger them, so the right people can respond.
  • We show ads and use limited device data for them — via Google AdMob — and use OneSignal to deliver push notifications. Neither is used to expose your location or circle activity to advertisers.
  • We never sell your personal information, and we never sell your location data.

1. Account & Setup

To use Family Location Tracker, you can either continue as an anonymous guest or sign in with your email address using a one-time passcode (no password is ever created or stored). Depending on how you use the App, your account may also include:

  • A display name, username, and avatar/initials you choose
  • The circle(s) you create or join, and your role within them (owner or member)
  • Preferences such as notification settings and whether your own location is shared with a given circle
  • Settings that control background location, battery-optimization exemption, Do Not Disturb bypass, and full-screen alert permissions

These settings let us operate the App's core safety and location-sharing features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • An account identifier created via our backend provider, Supabase
  • Your email address, if you choose to sign in with email (used only for the one-time passcode and account recovery — we never see or store a password)
  • If you continue as a guest, no email address, phone number, or real name is collected

2.2 Profile & Circle Data

To use circles, we store:

  • A display name, username, and avatar initials/color you choose
  • Circles you create or join, their join codes, and your membership/role within them
  • Invites and join requests you send or receive, and their approval status
This data is visible only to people you're connected to through a shared circle. Access rules on our backend (row-level security) prevent other users from reading your data unless you're actually in the same circle.

2.3 Location Data

If you are a member of a circle and choose to share your location with it, we collect and store, for as long as you remain a sharing member:

  • Precise (GPS) latitude and longitude, and general location derived from it (e.g., a reverse-geocoded address)
  • Heading, speed, and a basic driving-detection flag, used to show movement and a "driving" indicator to your circle
  • Device battery level and charging status, used for low-battery alerts to your circle
  • Online/offline status and the time of each location update
  • A location history used to show recent movement to your circle
  • Geofences (named areas) you or a circle member set up, and events generated when a device enters or leaves one

Location updates continue in the background (while the App is not open) so that your circle can see your current location and receive battery, geofence, and SOS alerts even when you're not actively using the App. You can stop sharing your location with a circle, or leave the circle, at any time from within the App; circle owners can also choose to hide their own location from a circle while still seeing other members' locations.

2.4 SOS & Ring Data

  • SOS alerts: When you trigger an SOS, we record your name/identifier, your current location, and the time of the alert, and send it in real time to the members of your circle so they can respond. The alert also records who resolves or cancels it.
  • Ring requests: When you or a circle member remotely rings a device to help locate it, we record who requested the ring, who the target device belongs to, and whether it was acknowledged.

2.5 Advertising & Device Information

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads. Google's SDK may collect and process data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • An advertising identifier and anonymous installation identifiers
  • Device type, model, operating system, and IP address
  • Ad interaction data

On iOS, we ask for your permission (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework) before using your advertising identifier for ad personalization. On Android, you can reset or limit use of your advertising ID in your device settings. This data is never based on your location, circle activity, or SOS/ring history.

2.6 What We Do Not Collect

  • No password — sign-in is passwordless (email one-time code) or fully anonymous
  • No government ID
  • No camera access or photos beyond an optional profile avatar image you choose to upload
  • No contacts list access
  • No microphone or audio recording
  • No payment card details — purchases are billed entirely through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, which do not share your card details with us

2.7 Push Notifications

We use OneSignal to deliver push notifications for SOS alerts, ring requests, battery alerts, geofence events, and invites. OneSignal receives a device push token and basic device information needed to deliver notifications; location-based OneSignal features are disabled in our configuration, so OneSignal does not separately collect your location.

2.8 Billing Information

Family Location Tracker offers optional premium features via subscription, managed through RevenueCat. Payment itself is processed entirely by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store — we do not directly collect or store your payment card details. RevenueCat and the app stores provide us with subscription status and anonymous purchase metadata (such as an app user identifier and entitlement status) so we can unlock premium features on your account.

5. Permissions

Family Location Tracker requests the following device capabilities. Each one exists to support the family-safety features described above:

  • Precise & Background Location: Required for the App's core purpose — sharing your location with your circle, showing movement/driving status, powering geofence alerts, and attaching your location to an SOS alert. Location continues to update while the App is in the background so your circle can see it and receive alerts even when you're not actively using the App. You can deny this permission, but doing so will prevent location sharing, geofencing, and location-based SOS from working.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver SOS, ring, battery, geofence, and invite alerts. Disabling this may prevent you from being notified of urgent events involving your circle.
  • Ignore Battery Optimization: Requested so background location tracking and alerts continue to work reliably; some phone manufacturers otherwise aggressively suspend background apps.
  • Bypass Do Not Disturb: Used so SOS and ring alerts can still make sound and appear even if your phone is on silent or in Do Not Disturb mode, similar to how phone calls behave.
  • Full-Screen Alerts / Display Over Other Apps (Android): Used to show an incoming SOS or "ring my device" alert over your lock screen, similar to an incoming call, so urgent alerts aren't missed.
  • Audio, Vibration & Wake Lock: Used to play the SOS/ring alarm sound, vibrate the device, and keep the device awake long enough to deliver an alert.
  • Foreground Service & Run at Startup: Used to keep location tracking and alerting running reliably, including automatically resuming after your device restarts.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to sync location, circle, and alert data, and to show ads.
Family Location Tracker does not request Camera, Contacts, or Microphone permissions. An optional profile avatar image can be uploaded by you, but the App does not access your camera roll or camera without your explicit action to do so.
