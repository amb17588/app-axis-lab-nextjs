Privacy Policy

Scope

Welcome to WiFi Manager's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how WiFi Manager ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our Wi-Fi diagnostics, security, and sharing mobile application and related services (the "App" or "Services").

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Overview

WiFi Manager helps you understand and manage the Wi-Fi networks you use — run instant speed tests and detect dead zones, share a network instantly via QR code, and get guidance on strengthening your network's security and managing connected devices and hotspots. WiFi Manager does not require you to create an account.

  • No account or sign-up — WiFi Manager does not ask for your name, email address, or a password, and it does not create any account for you.
  • Your Wi-Fi and diagnostics data stays on your device — network names, signal/speed-test results, and your saved security preferences are stored locally on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate a backend server for this data.
  • We show ads via Google AdMob — the App currently ships with Google's publicly documented test ad units while development continues; it will switch to real production ad units only once those are configured, behind a build-time flag (see Section 5 and Section 9).
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple/Google and RevenueCat — if you purchase a premium plan, billing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and subscription status is synced through our subscription-management provider, RevenueCat. We do not see or store your payment card details.
  • We never sell your personal information.

1. The App & How It Works

WiFi Manager is a utility app with three core areas, all used without an account:

  • Speed & diagnostics — run on-demand network speed tests and view signal-strength readings to help you find Wi-Fi dead zones.
  • QR sharing — generate or scan a QR code to share a Wi-Fi network's connection details with another device, using your device's own Wi-Fi credential store.
  • Security & hotspot guidance — guidance and settings to help you choose stronger passwords, review connected/unrecognized devices, and manage hotspots.

App preferences (such as units, notification choices, and onboarding progress) are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Wi-Fi & Diagnostics Data (stored only on your device)

The following is processed on your device to power the App's core features and is never sent to our servers:

  • Names (SSIDs) of Wi-Fi networks you scan, view, or save within the App
  • Signal-strength and speed-test readings
  • Security settings and preferences you configure in the App
  • QR codes you generate or scan for sharing a network, and the connection details they encode
This data is read using your device's own operating-system Wi-Fi APIs and is used entirely on-device to display diagnostics and sharing features back to you. We do not operate a server that stores this information.

2.2 Advertising & Device Information (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show native ads. Google's SDK may collect and process data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • An advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and anonymous installation identifiers
  • Device type, model, operating system, and IP address
  • Ad interaction data

The App currently uses Google's publicly documented test ad unit and App IDs while development continues, so no real ad personalization or measurement data is generated by this build yet. Production ad units are controlled by a build-time flag and will only be enabled once configured with our real AdMob account. This data is never based on the content of your Wi-Fi networks, diagnostics, or security settings.

2.3 Purchase & Subscription Data

If you purchase a premium plan, we use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, together with the Apple App Store or Google Play, which process billing. This involves:

  • An anonymous subscriber identifier generated automatically by RevenueCat
  • Purchase and subscription status (for example, which plan is active and its renewal date)

We do not directly collect or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

2.4 What We Do Not Collect

  • No account — no email address, phone number, real name, or password
  • No GPS or precise location tracking by us. (On some Android versions, the operating system itself requires the App to hold a location permission in order to return Wi-Fi scan results — this is an Android platform requirement, not something WiFi Manager uses to track your whereabouts, and no location data is transmitted to us.)
  • No camera access or photos, beyond rendering/scanning QR codes you choose to generate or scan, which never leaves your device
  • No contacts list access
  • No microphone or audio recording
  • No payment card details — purchases are billed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play

2.5 Device Data

  • Device type, model, and operating system
  • App version and language/region settings
  • An advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and anonymous installation identifiers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

5. Permissions

WiFi Manager may request the following device capabilities, depending on the feature you use and your OS version:
