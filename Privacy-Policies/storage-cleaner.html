Privacy Policy

AI Phone Storage Cleaner ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use the App to clean, optimize, and manage storage on your Android device.

1 Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require account creation. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

If you contact us for support:

  • Your email address
  • Any information you choose to provide

b. Device Storage & File Data

To scan and clean your device, the App reads:

  • File names, sizes, paths, and types of files stored on your device (photos, videos, audio, documents, APKs, cache, etc.)
  • Duplicate file information detected on your device
  • Installed app names, package names, sizes, and last-used timestamps (for app management features)
  • Storage usage statistics (used / free space on internal and external storage)
✅ This data is processed entirely on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate. It is not stored beyond your current session unless you explicitly save a scan report within the App.

c. App Preferences

The App stores on your device:

  • Scan history and saved reports
  • User preferences (theme, notification settings, scheduled scan times)
  • Onboarding completion status

d. Analytics & Crash Data

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) to collect:

  • Anonymized usage events (e.g., feature tapped, scan completed)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data to help us fix bugs
  • Device model, OS version, and app version (no personally identifiable information)
✅ This data is anonymized and aggregated. It does not identify you personally. You can opt out of analytics by disabling data sharing in the App's Settings.

2 How We Use Information

  • Scan your device storage to detect junk files, duplicates, large files, and unused apps
  • Perform cleaning and optimization operations you request
  • Schedule automatic smart scans (with your permission)
  • Send cleanup-reminder notifications (with your permission)
  • Remember your preferences and scan history
  • Improve the App through anonymized analytics
  • Fix bugs using crash reports
  • Respond to support queries

3 Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

READ / WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE To scan and delete files on your device storage.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / VIDEO / AUDIO To scan media files for duplicates and large files (Android 13+).
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE To access and clean all folders, including restricted ones (Android 11+).
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS To show last-used dates for installed apps so you can identify unused ones.
KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES To free RAM by stopping background app processes.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To send cleanup-reminder and scan-complete notifications.
VIBRATE / MODIFY_AUDIO For haptic and sound feedback during interactions.
WRITE_SETTINGS To apply certain device optimization settings.
⚠️ You may deny optional permissions, but some features may not function. The App will clearly explain why each permission is needed before requesting it.

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing:

6 Data Security

  • All file scanning and cleaning operations are performed locally on your device
  • No file content or file data is transmitted to our servers
  • Analytics data sent to Firebase is anonymized and encrypted in transit (TLS)
  • We practice minimal data collection — we only collect what is necessary

7 Data Retention

  • Scan history and preferences are stored on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it
  • Anonymized analytics data is retained by Firebase per Google's retention policy
  • Support emails may be retained for up to 2 years

8 Background Processing

The App may use Android's WorkManager / Background Task API to perform scheduled smart scans. These scans run on your device and do not transmit file data externally. You can disable scheduled scans in the App's Settings.
