Privacy Policy

Scope

Welcome to Speaker Cleaner's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how App Axis Lab ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our speaker-cleaning mobile application (the "App" or "Services").

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Overview

Speaker Cleaner plays sound tones and vibrations through your phone's speaker to help push out water and dust. It also offers speaker and headphone tests, cleaning tips, daily cleaning reminders, and optional rainy-weather alerts. No account is required. Your settings are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

  • No account is required — Speaker Cleaner works without sign-in, email, or any user profile.
  • Settings stay on your device — your language, reminder schedule, and rain-alert preferences are stored locally and never transmitted to our servers.
  • The App never records audio — the microphone is never used to listen to or record anything.
  • Location is optional and approximate — it is used only for Rain Alerts, only while the App is open, and is rounded to about 1 km before a weather forecast is requested.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob — Speaker Cleaner displays native ads. Google may collect certain data as described in Section 8.
  • We never sell your personal information.

1. What Speaker Cleaner Does

Speaker Cleaner is a speaker-cleaning utility for Android and iOS. It generates sound tones on your device and plays them, optionally together with vibration, through your phone's loudspeaker or earpiece to help push water and dust out of the speaker grille. It also includes a stereo speaker test, a headphone test, a tone generator, cleaning tips, daily cleaning reminders, and optional alerts when rain is forecast near you.

The App is not a repair tool and does not guarantee the removal of water or dust or the restoration of a damaged speaker. It is a cleaning aid only.

2. Information We Collect

Speaker Cleaner does not operate its own servers and does not transmit your settings or usage to us. All data the App creates stays on your device, except for the approximate location sent to the weather service (Section 2.2) and interactions with Google AdMob as described in Section 8.

2.1 App Settings

Your chosen preferences — app language, whether onboarding has been completed, cleaning reminder days and time, rain-alert preferences (timing, frequency, sound), and a short log of rain alerts already scheduled (so the same rain is not announced twice) — are stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us.

2.2 Approximate Location (Rain Alerts only)

If you turn on Rain Alerts and grant location permission, the App reads your device's approximate position when you open the App. It then:

  • Rounds the coordinates to two decimal places (about 1 km), so a precise location is never sent.
  • Requests an hourly rain forecast for that rounded position from Open-Meteo, a free weather service. No account, API key, or identifier of yours is included in the request.

Your location is used in memory only to fetch the forecast. We do not store, log, or share your location, and the App never accesses your location in the background. If Rain Alerts are turned off, the App does not access your location at all.

2.3 Generated Sounds

The cleaning and test tones are generated on your device and temporarily saved as audio files in the App's private cache so they can be played. They contain no personal data.

2.4 Billing Information

If you purchase a premium subscription or in-app purchase (when available), payment is processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. We may receive subscription status and anonymous purchase identifiers to unlock premium features.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Play cleaning tones and vibration patterns, and run the speaker, headphone, and tone tests
  • Show the App in your chosen language
  • Schedule daily cleaning reminders on the days and at the time you choose
  • Check the rain forecast near you and schedule rain alerts, if you turn Rain Alerts on
  • Remember your preferences between sessions
  • Process and manage premium purchases through the app stores

We do not use location, settings, or any other on-device data for advertising profiling, analytics, or automated decision-making.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as described in Section 2.2 (approximate location sent to Open-Meteo for the rain forecast), Section 7 (app store payments), and Section 8 (Google AdMob). We do not sell your personal information.

5. Permissions

Speaker Cleaner requests the following permissions:

  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Android; "While Using the App" — iOS): Used only for Rain Alerts, to fetch the rain forecast near you. The App does not request precise or background location. You may deny this; Rain Alerts will not work, but every other feature will.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Android 13+; Notifications — iOS): Used to show daily cleaning reminders and rain alerts. You may deny this; reminders and alerts will not be shown.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): Used by the vibration cleaning modes, which combine sound with vibration to help loosen water and dust.
  • Modify Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — Android): Used to route cleaning tones to the loudspeaker or the earpiece.
  • Microphone (iOS only): iOS requires this permission to play sound through the earpiece (the "Ear" option). Speaker Cleaner never records or listens to audio. The App does not request microphone access on Android.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used to fetch the rain forecast and to load ads.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING — Android): Used to offer premium purchases through Google Play.

6. Reminders & Rain Alert Notifications

Daily cleaning reminders and rain alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent from our servers and contain no personal data. The rain forecast is refreshed only when you open the App; there is no background refresh. You can turn reminders and alerts off at any time in the App's Settings.
